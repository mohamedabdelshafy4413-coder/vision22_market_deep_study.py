# Vision22 North America Growth Opportunity Study

Arabic-first Streamlit dashboard for Vision22's B2B market entry strategy in the United States and Canada.

## What this version contains

- Executive market-entry conclusion
- Published market evidence vs. Vision22 strategic estimates
- USA & Canada priority geographies
- 10 B2B target industries with decision makers and proposed offer ranges
- ICP & Buying Committee analysis
- Full detail for seven service packages
- Package ranking by 90-day sales potential, market opportunity, ease of close and price power
- 10 complete B2B outbound email templates
- 90-day go-to-market roadmap
- Interactive 90-day financial scenario model
- Full SWOT analysis
- Risk register and mitigation plan
- Launch-readiness checklist
- Compliance notes for US CAN-SPAM and Canada CASL
- Source and methodology section

## Important methodology note

Published market statistics are labeled **Market Evidence**. Package scores, proposed pricing, conversion assumptions and entry scores are **Vision22 Strategic Estimates** for planning purposes and are not guarantees or official market benchmarks.

## Run locally

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
streamlit run app.py
```
